## Project Overview

This project analyzes the performance of **Sunrise Coffee Chain**, a UK-based coffee retail business, using sales, store profitability, customer loyalty, product mix, and trading calendar data.

The objective was to move beyond surface-level dashboard reporting and identify practical business actions that can improve profitability, reduce store-level cost leakage, and strengthen revenue quality.

## Business Questions Answered

This analysis focuses on the questions a retail leadership team would care about:

- Which store formats generate the strongest profit margins?
- Which stores are consistently loss-making?
- Are high-revenue stores always profitable?
- Does higher rent translate into higher revenue or profit?
- Which stores improved profitability the most from 2023 to 2024?
- How do loyalty adoption, revenue mix, footfall, and average transaction value affect performance?
- Which trading periods, weekdays, weekends, and bank holidays drive or weaken revenue?
- Where should leadership focus first to improve margin?

### Summary of Findings

1. **Profit quality is uneven across the chain**  
   Overall profitability is healthy, but store-level performance varies significantly.

2. **Transport-Hub stores are small but highly efficient**  
   Transport-Hub locations show stronger margin performance than the wider High-Street estate.

3. **Some high-revenue stores are leaking profit**  
   Several stores generate strong sales but lose margin through rent, staffing, COGS, and operating cost pressure.

4. **Espresso is the core revenue engine**  
   Espresso-led categories contribute the largest share of daily revenue, while food is the second major commercial lever.

5. **Weekends and bank holidays are under-monetized**  
   Trading performance drops on weekends and UK bank holidays, creating an opportunity for targeted offers, staffing plans, and product mix optimization.

## Dataset Scope

The project includes:

- 60 stores
- 40 products
- Daily sales activity
- Monthly store profitability
- Store format, location, rent, staffing, loyalty, and product mix fields
- Trading calendar indicators including weekends and UK bank holidays

---

## Recommended Business Actions

- Build a **store turnaround list** using loss months, rent share, staffing intensity, and COGS share.
- Prioritize underperforming high-cost stores before expanding revenue campaigns.
- Use Transport-Hub economics as a benchmark for efficient store operation.
- Optimize weekend and bank holiday trading with targeted bundles and staffing plans.
- Use loyalty incentives to increase basket size, not only discount-driven transactions.
